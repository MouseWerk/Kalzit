## Improved

- Kalzit has its own icons: every icon in the app is redrawn in one language taken from the Kalzit
  mark, with octagons instead of circles, corners cut at an angle and rhombi for nodes and knobs.
  Icons with a surface (dashboards, data sources, the bell, people, files) carry one lightly tinted
  facet. The high contrast theme and the Windows contrast modes show the plain line only.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Open the sidebar, a dashboard toolbar and Verwaltung in the light, dark and high contrast theme:
  icons are sharp at 100 %, 125 % and 150 % display scaling, and the tinted facets are gone in high
  contrast and with a Windows contrast theme.
