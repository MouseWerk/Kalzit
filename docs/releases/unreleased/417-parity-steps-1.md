## Improved

- "Add step" opens a step catalog: search by name, description or a common term (rename, merge,
  top), the steps you added last, the table steps in six groups and a preview of each step on the
  first rows of your own result. Steps that do not fit your data yet are dimmed and say why.
- New table steps: Arrange and rename fields, Filter fields by name, Convert type, Filter rows,
  Limit, Stack tables, Series to rows and Reduce. They run in the browser and in exports alike.
- The Grafana import takes over organize, filter fields by name, filter data by values, limit,
  convert field type, reduce, merge and series to rows as steps and reports what it leaves out.
- Patterns in Filter fields by name and in the "matches" condition read the first 150 characters
  and refuse forms that could freeze a viewer's browser, with the reason shown while you type.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- In Explore on a SQLite source, open "Schritt hinzufügen", search "umbenennen" and "top", walk the
  list with the arrow keys and add a step with Enter; the preview on the right shows your rows.
