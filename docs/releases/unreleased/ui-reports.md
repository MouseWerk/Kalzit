## Improved

- Reports have their pages: "Reports" in the main navigation lists the reports of the organization
  with what they send, their schedule, format, recipients, next and last run and the person whose
  rights they run with; filters for your own reports and for those with problems; suspended and
  paused reports say so in words, and a suspended one names its reason.
- The report editor: dashboard or selected panels, time range and fixed variable values, a daily,
  weekly or monthly schedule or a cron expression with its time zone and the next runs, recipients
  by name or address, PDF, CSV or both, and the language. Errors show at their field, a refused
  recipient on the person the server names.
- A preview draws the draft as PDF on the server, with the data of now and your own rights, and
  sends nothing; "Send now" asks first, since it goes to every recipient.
- Each report lists its runs with trigger, duration, delivered recipients, size and the result with
  its reason.
- "Create report…" in a dashboard's menu starts a report with the time range and variable values
  chosen there, and the menu leads to the reports the dashboard already has.
- Without a mail server the report pages say so and lead server admins to Administration > Mail.
