## Improved

- The keyboard shortcuts help (press ? anywhere) is new: a search over meanings and key names, a list of areas, and the shortcuts that apply where you are shown first. ? opens and closes it, Esc clears the search first.
- The help now lists every shortcut of the app, also those of Explore (save, open), charts, graphs, traces, the analyses list, notification policies, Ask and the AI draft.
- Keys are named as on your keyboard in every language and on every platform: Strg, Umschalt, Eingabe, Leertaste and Entf in German; ⌃ ⌥ ⇧ ⌘ in the macOS order without “+”, and ⌫ for deleting on a Mac.
- Screen readers hear shortcuts by their names (“Control plus K”), and the time range buttons say their keys one after the other in their names.

## Fixed

- Redo in the query editors works with both Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+Shift+Z on every platform (⇧⌘Z and ⌘Y on a Mac).
- Shortcut hints in Explore, the graph and chart annotations show Cmd on a Mac instead of Ctrl.
- Holding down Ctrl+K, Ctrl+B or ? no longer makes the palette, sidebar or help flicker.
- On the analyses list, / always goes to the list's search, however the page was opened.
- On keyboards with a different letter order (German QWERTZ, French AZERTY) shortcuts follow the letter on the key: Ctrl+Y is redo, not undo, and Q no longer adds an annotation.
- The help also lists moving sign-in providers (Alt+Up and Down) and stopping an AI answer with Esc.
