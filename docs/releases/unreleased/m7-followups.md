## Improved

- The report of a Grafana import speaks your language: its notes are worded in German or English, and each item shows its title alone, with its kind in its own column.
- Alert rules on tables in long format show the name of the field (such as `amount`) instead of the internal label `__field__=amount`, in the alerting pages and in messages.
- Oracle: when a table was created or changed a moment before a query read it (ORA-01466), the error says so in plain words and suggests trying again in a moment.

## Fixed

- The backup guide shows the command that restores a container's data volume on readable lines again.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Import a Grafana dashboard with the UI in German: the report's notes are German and the items show their titles without "panel 2" or quotes.
