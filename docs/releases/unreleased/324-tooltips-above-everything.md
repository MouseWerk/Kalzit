## Fixed

- Hover tooltips of charts lie above everything now: the panel's edge no longer cuts them off,
  and neighbouring panels, sticky table headings, the dashboard toolbar and the top bar no longer
  cover them. They follow the pointer and flip or shift at the window's edges, so they stay fully
  visible.
- With a shared cursor that shows values, the tooltips of the other panels stay inside their own
  panel: they no longer cover the toolbar or a neighbouring panel, and a panel with too little
  room on screen shows only its cursor line.
- The tooltips of icon buttons and of the icon rail lie above the page as well and stay inside
  the window.
