## Improved

- Four more themes: Paper (warm off-white) and Calcite (cool blue-grey) as light variants,
  Graphite (almost black, for OLED screens) and Night (deep blue-grey) as dark variants. In
  Settings > Profile you pick the mode (System, Light, Dark, High contrast) and one light and one
  dark variant; System switches between the two with your device. The account menu, the command
  palette and the pages before sign-in offer the variants too. Nothing changes until you choose.
- Administration > Appearance and brand: besides the default mode, admins set the default light
  and dark variant for everyone without a choice of their own.
- The accent color "Graphite" is now called "Gray"; a stored choice stays as it is.
- A new custom accent color is checked against the surfaces of every variant, so a color that
  passes stays readable in each of them. A custom color chosen before keeps showing.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Settings > Profile: choose Paper and Night, set the mode to System, switch Windows between
  light and dark: the page follows without a reload and without a flash after a reload.
