## Improved

- Every page before the app (sign in, second factor, setup, create account, invitation, reset password, confirm email) has the same calm layout: one narrow column without a card, one heading size, at most one message, one main action and the side paths below.
- Sign-in providers stand above the password form, the first one as the main action; the password form is folded while providers are there.
- Results such as "Check your inbox", "Your password is changed" or an expired invitation are whole pages with one sentence and one action, instead of small tinted notices.
- Waiting after failed attempts is called a pause and says how long it lasts; "locked" now only means an account locked by an admin.
- The checkbox under the password is now "Shared or borrowed computer".
- An invitation shows who invited you, the role, the team and how long it is valid.
- Error pages stand in the same frame as the sign-in pages.
