## Improved

- Scrollbars are thin and quiet everywhere (pages, panels, tables, menus, editors and dialogs) and fit every theme in light and dark. They show stronger while the pointer is over the area that scrolls.
- On wider screens only the page area next to the sidebar scrolls: the sidebar, the top bar and the dashboard toolbar stay in place, and the page no longer shifts sideways when its content grows longer than the window.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Open a long dashboard and a long settings page: only the area next to the sidebar scrolls, the top bar stays, and nothing moves sideways while content loads.
- Scroll with the mouse wheel, PageDown, Space, Home and End right after opening a page; go to another page and back with the browser's Back button: the page returns to where it was.
- Check the scrollbars in Light, Papier, Kalzit, Dark, Graphit, Nacht and High contrast.
