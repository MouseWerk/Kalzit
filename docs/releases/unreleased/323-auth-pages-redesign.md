## Improved

- Sign in, the second step, password and invitation links and setup: on wide screens the form stands on the left and a panel on the right carries the backdrop. The admin's sign-in notice is now a card of its own in that panel, level with the heading (above the form on phones), instead of a small grey box. A notice longer than the window is shown in full: the page scrolls to its end.
- Settings > Appearance and brand: "Product line before sign-in" turns the line about Kalzit in that panel off. A server with its own product name or logo never shows the line.
- Sign in: the name of a sign-in provider that is too long for its button wraps inside the card.
