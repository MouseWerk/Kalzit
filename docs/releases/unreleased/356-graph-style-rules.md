## Improved

- Graph panels get style rules: under **Style** in the panel editor and in the panel head, rules
  set color, size, shape, icon, frame, label and badge of nodes, and color, width, line style,
  label and arrow of edges, always, on a condition or per unique value of a property. The lower
  rule wins, each rule reads as a sentence with its matches, and the legend names every rule.
- **Take over rules from another panel** copies the rules of another panel on the dashboard.
- Node and edge tables made from a graph show the same rules: a colored dot, icon, badge and label.
- About 360 icons for nodes, from Lucide (ISC), listed in the third-party notices.
