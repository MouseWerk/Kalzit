## Improved

- Kalzit's guide is now inside the app: **Help > Handbook** opens the handbook with every page of
  the guide in English and German, the operator pages (offline updates, several servers, winget)
  and the glossary. It is part of the program, so it works without internet and always matches
  the installed version.
- The handbook follows the language of the interface; a page without a German version shows the
  English one and says so. "Read in English" and "Auf Deutsch lesen" switch a single page.
- **Search the handbook** finds sections in both languages, with the sentence each word was found
  in, word forms and umlauts included ("kuerzester" finds "kürzester"). The search runs in your
  browser; what you type is never sent anywhere.
- Every page has contents, "On this page", previous and next, links to each section, code blocks
  with **Copy**, the guide's screenshots and notes and warnings. Some pages open the screen they
  describe, such as **Open Administration > Backups**, for people who may open it.
- The guide links of the app lead into the handbook instead of GitHub: the beginner guides in
  Explore and its cheat sheet, **Administration > Backups** and **Administration > Provisioning**.
  **Help > Kalzit on GitHub** stays as the one link outside; **Help > Documentation** is gone.
- Server admins can turn the handbook off under **Administration > Appearance** with **Show the
  handbook**; the Help menu then keeps only the keyboard shortcuts, and guide links open GitHub.
- New shortcuts in the handbook: **/** searches it, **[** and **]** turn to the previous and next
  page.
