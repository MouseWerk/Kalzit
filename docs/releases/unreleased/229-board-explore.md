## Improved

- Explore looks like its board: documents get the tabs "Documents", "Schema" and "Table
  (projection)", with how many came back and how long the query took beside them; "Share" copies
  a link to the query on screen for people in your organization; steps are compact cards that
  open on a click, and one "Add step" button lists the kinds of steps.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- In Explore on a MongoDB source, run a query, open "Table (projection)" and choose "Project into
  a table": a step appears and the table shows the fields. Choose "Share", paste the link into
  another Edge window signed in as another editor: the same query opens and runs.
