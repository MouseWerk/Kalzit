## Improved

- Invitations, password links, address confirmations, sign-up, second-factor and break-glass
  notices, the LDAP sync notice and the test mail now share one calm design: a header with the
  product name, one heading, the facts as a short list, at most one button with the link written
  out below it, a box with how long a link works and what to do if it was not you, and a short
  footer. Mails use your product name and accent color, come in a light and a dark version, and
  load nothing from the network; the plain-text part is wrapped for reading and never breaks a
  link.
- Settings > Email has a new switch "Mark in the mail header": it shows the mark or your own logo
  as an image carried inside the mail, or the name alone as text.
