## Improved

- Icons move a little: navigation and toolbar icons play a short motion on hover and keyboard focus, and give way when pressed.
- A successful connection test, a copy and a new favorite show their check or star once; a rule that starts firing rings the Alerting bell once.
- While a test, a check or a dashboard refresh runs, its refresh icon turns, and the sign-in and setup pages use the same turning icon.
- With reduced motion turned on in the system, every icon stands still.
