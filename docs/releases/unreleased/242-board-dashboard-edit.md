## Improved

- Dashboard edit mode matches its design: an "Editing" tag after the dashboard's name instead of a
  status line, the buttons "Settings", "Finish editing" and "Save dashboard", and an "Add panel or
  row" button at the end of the canvas that opens the Add pane.
- Panel shortcuts while editing: E opens the selected panel in the editor, Ctrl+D duplicates it,
  Ctrl+C copies it, Ctrl+V pastes a copied panel (also into another dashboard, or from the Add
  pane) and Delete deletes it (Backspace too on a Mac, where Cmd takes the place of Ctrl).
  Duplicating, pasting and deleting offer Undo, the result is
  announced to screen readers, the keys do nothing while you type in a field or query editor, and
  the shortcut help lists them.
- The inspector's own time range for a panel sits behind an "Override time range" switch; turning
  it off and on again while the panel stays selected brings the values back.
- The dashboard rail names its tools as on the design: "Filters and variables", "Annotations
  on/off", "Share and copy link", "Export (JSON)", "Versions and history", "Dashboard info" and
  "Dashboard settings".

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- In Edge and Firefox on Windows, edit a dashboard, select a panel and press Ctrl+D: the panel is
  duplicated and the browser does not open its bookmark dialog.
