## Improved

- Graph panels have tidier tools: zoom as icons, "Show all", Graph and Table as one switch, and
  the explorer with its icon. A hierarchy offers its direction (top to bottom or left to right)
  right in the panel.
- An empty graph result and a hierarchy too large to lay out offer "Edit query" to those who may
  edit the dashboard.
- Paths show their total weight when every edge carries a weight, cost or distance.
- The legend under a graph says what the node sizes stand for, and the panel names the selected
  node with Esc to clear it. Undo, pin and "Show only the selection" show their keys.
- "Add step" in Explore opens a searchable catalog that says what each step does and which shape
  goes in and out.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Open a dashboard with a hierarchy panel, switch its direction to "Left to right": the layers
  run from left to right. Open a network panel on a query without results as an editor: "Edit
  query" opens the panel editor; as a viewer the button is not there.
