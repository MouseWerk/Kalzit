## Improved

- Graph panels: drag a node to move it; a dragged node stays pinned where you dropped it. While viewing, your pinned nodes, their places and what you hid are kept for you and are back after a reload; in the panel editor, pinned nodes and their places are saved with the panel for everyone. "Release all" and "Back to the panel's state" undo them.
- Graph panels: an info card shows a node's or an edge's caption, labels and up to four properties after the pointer rests on it, or with I for the focused element. The panel editor chooses the properties per label.
- Graph panels: click an edge to select it and open its own detail sheet, with its ends, its typed properties and the other edges between the same two nodes.
- Graph panels: context menus for a node, an edge, the selection and the empty canvas (right click, Shift+F10 or the menu key).
- Graph panels: select with a rectangle or a lasso, invert the selection, hide nodes and edges and show them again; the line above the graph says how much is visible.
- Graph panels: search on the canvas in captions, properties and edges, and jump through the hits.
- Graph panels: a minimap, and Undo and Redo for changes of the view (moving, pinning, hiding, explorer steps, groups) with a history of the last 50 steps.
- Graph panels: the canvas tools (pointer, rectangle, lasso, search, undo, redo and zoom) lie on the canvas; every new action has a key, listed under "Graph" in the keyboard help (?).
- Graph panels: the table view lists what you hid, pins and releases nodes per row, selects edges and filters its rows with a search.
