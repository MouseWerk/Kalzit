## Improved

- The sidebar slides smoothly when you collapse it to the icon rail or open it again, instead of jumping. With reduced motion turned on, it switches at once as before.
- Collapsed, the sidebar slides out over the page while you rest the pointer on it, and slides back when you leave.
- Drag the sidebar's edge to set its width; drag it narrow to collapse it, or all the way to the window's edge to hide it.
- Focus mode (Ctrl+Shift+B, or "Focus mode" in the command palette) hides the sidebar completely; rest the pointer on the window's left edge to bring it in.
- Pinned dashboards stay in the collapsed sidebar as letter tiles.
- Groups of sub-pages (for example under Administration) have a clear heading with a line above them.
