## Improved

- Konto > Anmeldung und Sicherheit is laid out as two cards, "Anmeldung" and "Zweiter Faktor",
  with the provider or directory you sign in through ("Verknüpfte Anmeldung"), a summary of your
  methods, renaming a passkey with "Speichern" and "Abbrechen" in the row, and "Einrichten" next
  to the hint when you have no second factor yet.
- Verwaltung > Anmeldung: "Anbieter hinzufügen" sits in the page header, the figures appear on
  the "Anbieter" tab as separate tiles and now include the share of people with a second factor.
  On "Lokale Anmeldung und 2FA" the checks sit in a column beside the settings, the allowed
  methods are two boxes, and the rule per organization is part of the second factor's card.
- Mitglieder is a table with team, sign-in (provider or password), second factor and last
  activity per person; less frequent actions such as "Passwort-Link senden", "Zweiten Faktor
  zurücksetzen…" and "Entfernen…" are in the row's menu. Open invitations are a table with how
  many days each link is still valid.
- Dienstkonten: each account and the SCIM card carry their symbol, the details close with an X,
  and revoking and deleting read in the danger tone.
- Invitation and password-reset links that expired, were used or are unknown say so in a notice
  with one clear button; signed in with another account, "Abmelden und annehmen" is the main
  button.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Open Konto > Anmeldung und Sicherheit with an account that signs in through a provider: the
  "Anmeldung" card names the provider and "Verwaltet von …".
- Open Verwaltung > Mitglieder: every row shows team, sign-in, second factor and "Zuletzt aktiv";
  the "…" menu removes a member after asking.
