## Improved

- Verwaltung > Mitglieder: the e-mail address of a member shows as a tooltip only while it is
  actually cut with an ellipsis; an address that fits no longer repeats itself in a tooltip.
- Reports and figure tiles: the lines under a report's name and the label of a figure tile show
  their full text as a tooltip only while they are cut, in the same way.
- Verwaltung > Plugins: the page of an app says how an app runs, in a sealed-off frame in the
  browser with the rights of the person signed in, instead of the text for a panel, and no longer
  shows an empty "Result shapes" row.
- Verwaltung > Sicherungen: the three tiles for the last backup, the next one and the target look
  like the figure tiles of the other pages, and their values start at the same height.
