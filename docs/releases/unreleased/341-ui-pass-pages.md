## Fixed

- Members: on a laptop-sized window the role of each member is readable again instead of cut to
  its first letter, and the e-mail address stays on one line; an address longer than its column
  ends in an ellipsis and shows in full as a tooltip. Where the list is too narrow for all its
  columns, the second factor stands under the way of signing in. A member of many teams lists the
  first two on at most two lines and counts the rest; the count is a button that shows all of
  them in the row, with the keyboard and on a touch screen too.
- Reports: the list keeps its rows short on a laptop-sized window. The report's name takes the
  free width and the other columns stay on one line each; where the list is too narrow, format
  and creator stand under the report's content, and on a phone every report is a block of
  labelled lines instead of a table cut off at the side. A search without a match shows one line
  and no empty table, and the search field no longer cuts its own placeholder.
- Dashboards and Explore: the filters and the toolbar share one row where the window has room
  for both. In Explore a long data source name ends in an ellipsis instead of pushing the tools
  to a second row, and Open, History, Share and Cheat sheet are icon buttons with tooltips on
  narrower windows.
- Explore: suggestions of the query editor close when you leave the editor; they no longer open
  over the result after a click on Run.
- The bar at the top says where you are on every page: the settings show "Settings" and the
  section (and the plugin below it), Explore shows "Explore" and the saved analysis that is open,
  and the data source list, its trash and the administration overview name themselves too.
- Start, "Recently changed": changes to alert rules, contact points, notification policies,
  silences, alerting settings, reports, public links, snapshots and provisioning read as full
  sentences with the name of what changed ("Olga created the report “Weekly sales”"), like the
  older entries. Public links and snapshots name their dashboard where you may see it.
- The audit log names the actions on public links and snapshots in words instead of showing
  their technical names.
- Report pages: the notes, the tabs and the form keep the same distance from each other.
- A dashboard filter with a short value keeps it readable where the row is tight.
- On a phone the bar at the top shows the page and the one above it on one line, also for a
  dashboard snapshot, instead of wrapping a long trail over several lines.
- The SCIM figures on the service accounts page use the same tiles as Start: a white card, a
  quiet label and the number.
- Panel editor on a phone: the heading "Panel bearbeiten" is no longer cut; the tools beside it
  move to the next row.
- A playing playlist on a phone: the buttons stand below the dashboard's filters instead of
  running into them.
