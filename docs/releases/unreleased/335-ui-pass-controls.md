## Fixed

- Placeholder texts are readable in every theme: search fields, the question field of Ask and
  the plugin and catalog searches no longer show the browser's faint grey.
- Switches, the check boxes of the data source list, the remove marks of chips (recipients, labels,
  excluded fields, context) and the alert badge on a panel answer on an area of at least
  24 x 24 px, so they are easier to hit with a finger or a shaky pointer. They look as before.
- A row with a switch or a check box is at least 24 px high, so its label is an easy target too
  ("Active" in the alert rule editor, "This is not my browser" when signing in).
- The plugin pages (trust, catalog, the organization's plugins) show the same switch as the rest of
  the app instead of a small check box.
- The accent swatches and "Custom…" stand on one line of one height in the appearance settings, and
  so do "Dashboard as context" and "Send" below a question.
- A select whose value is cut off shows the whole value as a tooltip, however short the value is
  (a role shown as "O…" in a narrow table); a pinned dashboard with a long title does the same in
  the sidebar.
