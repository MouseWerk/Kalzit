## Fixed

- Alerting: the lists of rules, contact points and silences no longer scroll the whole page
  sideways on a phone or in a narrow window. On a phone each row is a small card with the
  column's name in front of its values.
- Alert rules: in a window of medium width the rows are much shorter. The data source and the
  shape stand under the rule's name, the interval under the condition, and the state, "silenced
  until" and the interval no longer wrap.
- Notification policies: the last column writes what a policy sets itself on one line and says
  "inherited" once for the rest, instead of four stacked values with "inherited" up to four
  times. On a phone each policy is a small card.
- Notification policies and the other alerting lists: the same distance between the tabs,
  notices, the explanation and the table.
- Alert rule editor: the choices for shape, function and the way of notification are as wide as
  their options instead of the whole card, and the preview starts on the same line as the form.
- Alert rule editor: the "Active" switch and "Label" are easier to hit and line up with the other
  fields; the buttons that add a label, a condition or an escalation step look the same in the
  rule, the policy and the silence.
- Alert history: an empty range shows one line instead of a table header over nothing.
- The alert badge in a panel's title bar and the fold button of a policy are easier to hit.
