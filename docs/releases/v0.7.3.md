# Kalzit 0.7.3

A security update for alerting from the two reviews of 0.7.2: alert groups keep data sources
apart, the alerting history shows nothing of data sources you may not read, and notification
policies stay fast and within their limits.

## Fixed

- Alerting groups: rules of different data sources no longer share a group, even with the same
  labels. A group with alerts from a data source you may not read or query is hidden as a whole
  and shows only its state and counts, without when it started, began firing or last notified;
  your own groups stay visible and are no longer affected by such alerts. Open groups move to the
  new grouping when the server starts and keep their repetitions, escalation steps and resolves.
- Alerting history: a group's notifications for alerts from a data source you may not read or
  query no longer appear, and a silence that matched such an alert is no longer named. Who sees an
  entry now follows the data source its rule had when the entry was written; moving a rule to
  another data source ends its open alerts and sends their resolves.
- Contact points: when a contact point last delivered is shown only to people who manage contact
  points.
- Slack and Teams messages no longer turn addresses in rule names, labels or errors into
  clickable links; only Kalzit's own buttons link.
- Notification policies are no longer rebuilt for every evaluation and page view: their regular
  expressions are compiled once until the policies change, so large policy trees keep evaluations
  and the policy pages fast, and a change on another server of a cluster still applies at once.
  The size limit of a regular expression stays 64 KiB.
- "Weg prüfen" checks each label set against the label limits of the data, answers at most 500
  ways (with a note when the answer was cut) and allows 120 checks a minute per person; the rule
  editor checks once typing pauses.
- Creating, changing or moving a notification policy while another one changes can no longer go
  past the depth limit or leave a repeat interval shorter than the group interval it inherits.
- Rules with many instances in large groups are stored faster after each evaluation.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Send an alert whose rule name and labels hold `https://example.com/x`, `www.example.com` and
  `host.example.com` to a Slack and a Teams channel: the addresses read normally but are not
  links, and the buttons still open Kalzit.
- Update a 0.7.2 installation with a firing, escalated alert group: after the update the group
  keeps its escalation step, sends no second firing message, and its resolve reaches every
  contact point that got the alert.
