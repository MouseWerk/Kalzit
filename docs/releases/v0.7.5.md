# Kalzit 0.7.5

0.7.5 brings the screens for traces, CORS, self-registration, backups and app plugins, limits that
keep snapshots and public links from straining the server, and a pass over the whole interface:
lists that fit laptops and phones, controls that are easier to hit, and tooltips only where text
is cut.

## Improved

- Traces in Explore: a search form for Tempo and Jaeger without a query language (service,
  operation, tags, duration, status), which shows the TraceQL it stands for, the result list with
  duration over time, and one trace as a waterfall with the detail of a span (attributes, events,
  linked spans, resource). Bars are neutral; only errors are red, and say so in words. The detail
  of a span stands beside the waterfall, also at 1024 px; on a phone it opens as a sheet over the
  waterfall and gives the focus back to the span's row when it is closed.
- Trace panel for dashboards: the list of traces, the waterfall of one, or automatically by what
  the query answers; a click sets a variable. A list of traces on a phone or in a small panel
  shows id, root, duration and status instead of scrolling sideways, and a trace in a low panel or
  in the panel editor's preview scrolls in its frame.
- Trace steps in the step catalog: filter spans, critical path, spans or traces as a table,
  service map and span tree, with a question before a graph over very many spans.
- Loki, Tempo and Jaeger: label, tag and service names are offered while typing a query.
- Alert rules on tables in long format show the name of the field (such as `amount`) instead of
  the internal label `__field__=amount`, in the alerting pages and in messages.
- The report of a Grafana import speaks your language: its notes are worded in German or English,
  and each item shows its title alone, with its kind in its own column.
- Oracle: when a table was created or changed a moment before a query read it (ORA-01466), the
  error says so in plain words and suggests trying again in a moment.
- Settings > CORS: origins, methods and headers apply at once with undo, a wrong entry says why
  at its field, and an origin can be checked against the settings. The lists of origins and
  headers stand under their text at one width.
- Settings > Registration, with the pages for visitors (create an account, check the inbox,
  name and password behind the link, waiting for approval) and the requests above the members.
  "Approve" and "Refuse" stand on one line as quiet buttons, and "Create account" names the
  allowed domains as "a or b" in the page's language.
- Settings > Backups: schedule, retention, encryption and the last runs, and the target
  (local folder or S3-compatible) with a test before the one Save. The target stands in one place
  and is edited from the section's header; the last runs are a list with sizes and durations at
  the right edge, one block per run on a phone, and the page fits 1024 px and phones without
  scrolling sideways.
- Apps: installed apps appear under "Apps" in the sidebar and run in a sealed frame; an app with
  several pages shows its name once and its pages below it by their own titles, and its page says
  where it is in the top bar ("Apps / name"). An app carries the same two letters everywhere.
  Settings > Plugins shows an app's pages, what it may do and how it runs (in a sealed-off frame
  in the browser with the rights of the person signed in), and Settings > Apps blocks an app or
  limits it to a role per organization.
- Figures on Home, Administration > Service accounts (SCIM), Administration > Shares,
  Administration > Provisioning, the import report and Settings > Backups all use the same tile:
  a card, a quiet label and the value, starting at the same height.
- Members, reports and figure tiles show their full text as a tooltip only while it is actually
  cut with an ellipsis; text that fits no longer repeats itself in a tooltip.

## Fixed

- Placeholder texts are readable in every theme: search fields, the question field of Ask and
  the plugin and catalog searches no longer show the browser's faint grey.
- Switches, the check boxes of the data source list, the remove marks of chips (recipients,
  labels, excluded fields, context) and the alert badge on a panel answer on an area of at least
  24 x 24 px, so they are easier to hit with a finger or a shaky pointer. A row with a switch or a
  check box is at least 24 px high, so its label is an easy target too.
- The plugin pages (trust, catalog, the organization's plugins) show the same switch as the rest
  of the app instead of a small check box.
- A select whose value is cut off shows the whole value as a tooltip, however short the value is;
  a pinned dashboard with a long title does the same in the sidebar.
- The accent swatches and "Custom…" stand on one line of one height in the appearance settings,
  and so do "Dashboard as context" and "Send" below a question.
- The bar at the top says where you are on every page: the settings show "Settings" and the
  section (and the plugin below it), Explore shows "Explore" and the saved analysis that is open,
  and the data source list, its trash and the administration overview name themselves too. On a
  phone it shows the page and the one above it on one line, also for a dashboard snapshot.
- Home, "Recently changed": changes to alert rules, contact points, notification policies,
  silences, alerting settings, reports, public links, snapshots and provisioning read as full
  sentences with the name of what changed, like the older entries. Public links and snapshots
  name their dashboard where you may see it. The audit log names the actions on public links and
  snapshots in words instead of their technical names.
- Dashboards and Explore: the filters and the toolbar share one row where the window has room
  for both. In Explore a long data source name ends in an ellipsis instead of pushing the tools to
  a second row, and Open, History, Share and Cheat sheet are icon buttons with tooltips on
  narrower windows. A dashboard filter with a short value keeps it readable where the row is
  tight.
- Explore: suggestions of the query editor close when you leave the editor; they no longer open
  over the result after a click on Run.
- The menu of a dashboard panel low in the window opened under its button, with Edit, Duplicate
  and Delete out of reach. It now opens above the button where there is more room, and scrolls in
  itself in a low window.
- Panel editor on a phone: the heading is no longer cut; the tools beside it move to the next
  row. A playing playlist on a phone shows its buttons below the dashboard's filters instead of
  running into them.
- Data sources: a closed Cache section sums up a switched-on cache as "Query cache" instead of the
  whole sentence of its switch, which was cut off in German. The full wording shows as a tooltip
  and stays in the section itself.
- Alerting: the lists of rules, contact points and silences no longer scroll the whole page
  sideways on a phone or in a narrow window; on a phone each row is a small card with the
  column's name in front of its values.
- Alert rules: in a window of medium width the rows are much shorter. The data source and the
  shape stand under the rule's name, the interval under the condition, and the state, "silenced
  until" and the interval no longer wrap.
- Notification policies: the last column writes what a policy sets itself on one line and says
  "inherited" once for the rest; on a phone each policy is a small card that names each time
  ("wait 30 s"). The fold button of a policy is easier to hit, and all alerting lists keep the
  same distance between the tabs, notices, the explanation and the table.
- Alert rule editor: the choices for shape, function and the way of notification are as wide as
  their options, the preview starts on the same line as the form, "Label" lines up with the other
  fields, and the buttons that add a label, a condition or an escalation step look the same in
  the rule, the policy and the silence.
- Alert history: an empty range shows one line instead of a table header over nothing.
- Reports: only the person who created a report and organization admins can change, resume, send
  or delete it, since it runs and mails with its creator's rights. Others who may write reports
  can still pause it; the list, the editor and the runs page show only what each person may do.
- Reports: the list keeps its rows short on a laptop-sized window. The report's name takes the
  free width and the other columns stay on one line each; where the list is too narrow, format
  and creator stand under the report's content, and on a phone every report is a block of
  labelled lines. A search without a match shows one line and no empty table, and the search
  field no longer cuts its own placeholder. The notes, the tabs and the form of the report pages
  keep the same distance from each other.
- Snapshots can no longer fill the server's disk: an organization keeps at most 500 snapshots
  taking 1 GB together (both adjustable under Settings > Sharing), one person makes at most
  10 snapshots a minute (per server, when Kalzit runs on several), and an internal snapshot made
  without an expiry now stays 7 days instead of 30. Revoking a snapshot deletes what it stored at
  once and frees its room; expired snapshots count until they are deleted 30 days later. A
  refusal says what to do: revoke snapshots nobody needs, or wait a moment.
- Visitors of public links no longer use up the query slots of the person who made the link:
  their panels run in slots of their own per organization on each server. When those are busy a
  panel waits a moment, the page tries again by itself, and says the server is busy if it stays
  that way. One link's visitors can run only so many different time ranges a minute.
- Opening a public link or snapshot no longer writes to the database on every page load: views
  are counted in memory and written every few seconds.
- The snapshot list no longer shows a snapshot's title to someone who may not read its
  dashboard, since the title is the dashboard's by default.
- A snapshot inside the organization fits a phone again: it lines up with every other page, and
  its title, time range and the way back to the live dashboard wrap instead of being cut off.
- A panel that had no result when a snapshot was made now says so, in the app and on the public
  snapshot, instead of suggesting it will load later. The Share dialog names the panels that have
  no result right now before a snapshot is made.
- Administration > Provisioning keeps each file path on one line, shortened in the middle with the
  full path as its tooltip; the resource name gets the free width, and on narrow screens each
  resource is a card. The tile for sets shows their number and the set applied last instead of
  listing every set.
- `kalzit provision`: a secret file (`fromFile`) is read only from the set's folder, or from
  folders you name with `--secret-dir` (such as `/run/secrets`; also
  `KALZIT_PROVISION_SECRET_DIRS`). It must be a regular file with one name, at most 64 KiB. `..`,
  links that lead out of those folders, devices and system paths such as `/proc` are refused, and
  a secret that holds the value of one of Kalzit's own `KALZIT_*` variables is never sent.
- Administration > Members: on a laptop-sized window the role of each member is readable again
  instead of cut to its first letter, and the e-mail address stays on one line, ending in an
  ellipsis when it is longer than its column. Where the list is too narrow for all its columns,
  the second factor stands under the way of signing in. A member of many teams lists the first
  two on at most two lines and counts the rest; the count is a button that shows all of them in
  the row, with the keyboard and on a touch screen too.
- Settings > Plugins: the page of an app no longer shows an empty "Result shapes" row.
- The mail that tells server admins about a waiting registration linked to a page that does not
  exist; it now opens the members page, where the requests are decided.
- The backup guide shows the command that restores a container's data volume on readable lines
  again.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- Import a Grafana dashboard with the UI in German: the report's notes are German and the items
  show their titles without "panel 2" or quotes.
- Run `kalzit.exe provision plan` with a `fromFile` secret in the set's folder (it is read), and
  with one outside that folder and outside `--secret-dir` (it is refused).
