# Kalzit 0.7.2

Alerting routing: notification policies send alerts by their labels, group several rules into
one message, escalate groups that keep firing, and reach Slack and Microsoft Teams.

## Improved

- New page "Policies" in Alerting, between Rules and Contact points: a tree of notification
  policies from "Standard" down. A policy matches labels with `=`, `!=`, `=~` and `!~`; the first
  matching policy takes the instance, and "continue" lets it take further ways. Values a policy
  leaves empty are inherited from above and shown as "inherited". Organization admins add, edit,
  duplicate, delete and move policies (row menu, Alt and the arrow keys, or dragging); every move
  is saved at once and can be undone. Everyone who may read alert rules sees the tree read-only.
- Instances of several rules with the same labels arrive together in one message per group, after
  a group wait (30 seconds by default), at most once per group interval (5 minutes), and as a
  repetition while they keep firing (every 4 hours, or off).
- Up to three escalation steps per policy send a group that keeps firing to further contact
  points, for example after 15 minutes to the on-call team. A silence holds the escalation.
- Slack and Microsoft Teams as contact points, with formatted messages in German or English. The
  channel's incoming webhook URL is stored encrypted and afterwards shown only by host and its
  last four characters; "Replace" sets a new one. The form shows what a message looks like and
  sends a test after saving.
- Rule editor, step 5: "Through policies" or "Directly to contact points". Through policies, the
  editor shows the way of each instance from "Evaluate now"; the message preview shows mail,
  Slack and Teams. Rules with contact points from 0.7.0 stay direct, so the update changes no
  delivery.
- "Check way" shows which policies typed labels, or a rule's instances, reach, with contact
  points, grouping and times, and why a matching policy is not reached.
- The rules list has a column "Way" with the policy path, "+1 way" for further ways, and "Direct"
  for rules that go straight to their contact points.
- The history names, for each delivery of a group, its policy and stage: the change, a
  repetition, an escalation step, or a resolve because the way changed.
- Silences can also use `!~`; for rules routed through policies they also see the rule's labels,
  direct rules match as before.
- Webhooks of rules routed through policies send `version: 2` with the group and every instance's
  rule; direct rules keep `version: 1`. Both stay within the configured largest payload.
- People who may not read a rule's data source see its groups only as a count of hidden groups,
  without policy, labels or contact points, and the way of its instances only from the rule's own
  labels.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- In Alerting > Policies, add a policy below "Standard", move it with Alt+Up and undo the move,
  then check a way with typed labels.
- Create a Slack or Teams contact point with a real incoming webhook URL, send a test, check the
  message arrives formatted in the chosen language, and that only host and the last four
  characters of the URL are shown afterwards.
- Build a small tree ("Standard" with a contact point, one child for `team=lager` with an
  escalation step of 5 minutes), let two rules with `team=lager` fire, and check they arrive in
  one message, then the escalation after 5 minutes.
- Silence the firing group and check the escalation pauses; end the silence and check it
  continues.
- Update a server with 0.7.0 rules that have contact points and check they are listed as "Direkt"
  and keep notifying as before.
