# Kalzit 0.4.0

## Improved

- AI, off until a server administrator turns it on: under Settings > AI (Verwaltung > KI) connect
  a provider (any OpenAI-compatible server such as Ollama, LiteLLM or OpenAI, or Anthropic), test
  the connection and turn AI on. Without a tested provider the switch stays off, and while AI is
  off no AI button or hint appears anywhere.
- The AI page shows the providers with where their data goes, the usage per day and organization
  in calls and tokens, the limits per person and organization, and the text "Which data leaves the
  server" with a paragraph of your own.
- Organizations can turn AI off for their members, connect a provider of their own when the
  server allows it, and lower the limits (Settings > AI of the organization).
- Data sources have an AI part: "AI may read" (off by default) decides whether AI sees the
  source's schema and results, and "Excluded fields" keeps the values of named fields from ever
  reaching a provider. The data source list marks opened sources.
- Before the first AI call, everyone sees once per provider which data goes where.
- Explore: "Explain" explains the query in the editor, "Draft" writes a query from a description.
  A draft is never run by itself: "Apply" puts it into the editor, and running it checks it like
  any query.
- "Summarize" in Explore and in a panel's menu summarizes a result from sources opened to AI, and
  says what was sent; viewers can summarize the panels they see.
- Answers appear while they are written, can be stopped with Stop or Escape, and say when a limit
  is reached or a source is not open to AI.

## Checklist for Windows (manual)

- AI on Windows: run Ollama (or another OpenAI-compatible server) on the same machine, add it under
  Settings > AI with `http://localhost:11434/v1` and a pulled model, press "Test connection" and
  check that the answer and the time appear; turn "AI functions" on.
- Open a SQLite source to AI on its page ("AI may read"), add `email` to "Excluded fields", then
  in Explore run `SELECT * FROM customers LIMIT 5` on it, press "Summarize" and check that the
  notice appears once, that the summary streams in, and that the note says how many rows were
  sent; press "Explain" and "Draft", apply a draft and check that Ctrl+Z takes it back.
- Stop a running answer with Escape and check that the audit log shows the call as cancelled,
  without any query text.
