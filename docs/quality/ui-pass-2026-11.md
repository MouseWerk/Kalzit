# UI pass 2026-11: checklist, sweep and findings

The last step of M7: one pass over every screen for how it looks, not only whether it works. This
file holds the checklist the pass uses, how the automated sweep maps onto it, and the findings of
the sweep run of 2026-11-19 on `main` at `68548b74`, with a direction for each. Nothing is fixed
here: fixes follow as their own pull requests once the owner has seen the list. The screens of
#325 and #326 were still landing when the sweep ran and get their own run afterwards.

The boards in `docs/design/blueprint/` lead, `docs/design/feel.md` gives the principles, and the
tokens in `ui/src/styles/tokens.css` are the only source of colors, sizes and spacing.

## 1. Checklist

Each line can be answered with yes or no on a screen. "Sweep" names the automated check that
answers it (section 2); "eye" means a person looks at the screenshots.

### Hierarchy

| # | Check | How |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | One `h1` per page, in the page header; the dashboard, Explore and editor pages carry it in the toolbar row. Sections are `h2`, never a bold paragraph | eye |
| H2 | At most one filled accent button per view (the primary action). Everything else is secondary (hairline) or ghost. Destructive actions are never the filled one outside their confirmation | eye |
| H3 | Type comes from the scale only: 11, 12, 13, 14, 17, 20 px (`--font-size-2xs` to `-xl`), larger only for the Start greeting and stat numbers; weights 400 to 600 | eye |
| H4 | Muted text (`--text-muted`) is for secondary information only: never the one thing a row is about, never a field's value | eye |
| H5 | Color carries meaning (data, state, the primary action). Surfaces, frames and selected or current states stay neutral: no accent border, outline, glow or bar | sweep `state-frame` |

### Spacing and alignment

| # | Check | How |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Every gap is a step of the 4 px grid (`--space-*`); between panels and inside a panel frame 8 px, the canvas gutter 24 px (16 px on a phone) | eye |
| S2 | Page header, toolbar, content and table share one left edge; nothing starts 2 or 6 px off its neighbours | eye |
| S3 | Canvas pages (dashboards, Explore, lists) fill the width; forms, settings and text keep 72 rem (`--content-max-width`) | eye |
| S4 | Controls of one row have one height (`--control-height`: 32 px, 28 compact, 36 comfortable) and sit on one baseline | sweep `row-height` |
| S5 | Labels sit above their field with one gap; help text below with one gap; the same in every form | eye |
| S6 | Numbers in tables are right-aligned with tabular figures; text left; a column never changes its alignment between rows | eye |

### Controls

| # | Check | How |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | One shape per kind: buttons and fields 7 px radius (`--radius-md`), cards and panels 12 px, pills only for chips and the current nav item | eye |
| C2 | A button says what it does in one to three words; an icon-only button has an accessible name and a tooltip | sweep `name`, eye |
| C3 | No two controls overlap, and nothing is drawn over a control's label | sweep `overlap` |
| C4 | Pointer targets are at least 24 x 24 px, or have 24 px of room around their centre (WCAG 2.2, 2.5.8); the main actions of a phone layout at least 32 px high | sweep `target-size` |
| C5 | Keyboard focus is always visible, as the one neutral ring (`--focus-outline`, 2 px, offset 2 px), only on `:focus-visible` | sweep `focus` |
| C6 | Every control, field and meaningful image has an accessible name; a field is never named by its placeholder alone | sweep `name` |
| C7 | Disabled controls say why (a hint or tooltip) when the reason is not obvious from the screen | eye |

### Text

| # | Check | How |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | No text is cut off. Where a name may be long (titles, table cells, chips) it ends in an ellipsis and carries a `title`; labels, buttons, headings and tabs are never cut, they wrap or the layout gives way | sweep `clipped-text`, `truncated` |
| T2 | German fits: every screen holds in German, where labels run about a third longer ("Benachrichtigungsrichtlinien", "Datenquelle hinzufügen") | sweep in `de` |
| T3 | Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, large text (24 px, or 18.66 px bold) 3:1, in both themes, placeholders included | sweep `contrast` |
| T4 | Wording follows `docs/glossary.md`; German uses "du"; "Explore" stays "Explore" | eye |

### States

| # | Check | How |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Empty: one muted line that says what belongs here and, for those who may, the first step as a button. No bare table header over nothing | eye |
| E2 | Loading: the old content stays, with the thin bar at the top edge; no layout jump when the data arrives | eye |
| E3 | Error: says in plain words what happened and what to do, next to the thing that failed; the field's error under the field | eye |
| E4 | Nothing is written to the browser console on any page | sweep `console` |

### Widths and themes

| # | Check | How |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Nothing sticks out to the right and the page never scrolls sideways, at 390, 1024 and 1440 px | sweep `overflow` |
| W2 | At 390 px a toolbar wraps or moves into its overflow menu; tables stack or scroll inside their own frame | sweep `overflow`, eye |
| W3 | 1024 px is a full layout, not a squeezed 1440: the sidebar may fold to its rail, two-column forms may become one | eye |
| W4 | Dark is the same design: every surface, border and state that exists in light exists in dark, with the same order of layers (sidebar below the canvas, cards above) | sweep in `dark`, eye |

## 2. The sweep

`npm run e2e:sweep` (in `ui/`) starts the release binary the way the e2e tests do and opens every
page in light and dark, German and English, at 1440, 1024 and 390 px. It is run on demand and is
not a CI gate: a finding fails nothing. Only two things fail it: a route of the router that the
sweep does not know, and a check that no longer finds its probe.

- **Pages** (`ui/e2e/sweep/pages.ts`): the main pages of the layout and accessibility checks
  (`support/pages.ts`), every other address of the router with seeded data (a team, a role, an
  alert rule with contact point, policy and silence, a saved analysis, a playlist, a report), each
  section of the settings (read from `sections.ts`) and the pages before sign-in. The routes are
  read from the `createRoute` declarations (`support/routes.ts`). The screens of 0.7.5 have a
  file of their own (`ui/e2e/sweep/server-admin.ts`) with the answers a fresh server cannot give
  (a Tempo source, a mail server, a backup target with runs, an installed app).
- **Checks** (`ui/e2e/support/audit.ts`): `overflow` (the existing `support/overflow.ts`),
  `clipped-text` and `truncated`, `overlap`, `target-size`, `row-height`, `contrast` (computed
  colors, composited over what lies behind), `name`, `focus` (every control takes the keyboard
  focus in turn), `state-frame`, plus `console` (the existing console guard, drained per page)
  and `open` (a page that did not reach its state).
- **Report**: `ui/e2e/.output/ui-sweep/report.html` (each finding with a cut of the screenshot
  and the element marked), `report.md`, `report.json` and `shots/<variant>/<page>.jpg`.
- **Exceptions** (`ui/e2e/sweep/allow.ts`): each with its reason; allowed findings stay in the
  report in a list of their own.
- `KALZIT_SWEEP_PAGES="alert|report"` narrows a run to some pages,
  `-- --grep "dark, de, 390"` to some variants.

What the sweep cannot see and a person has to: H1 to H4, S1 to S3, S5, S6, C1, C7, T4, E1 to E3,
W3. Section 4 holds those from the screenshots of this run.
